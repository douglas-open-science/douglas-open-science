# Open methods

Open methods include diverse open science practices related to sharing – in publicly accessible forums and formats – the open software processes, procedures, and materials used in research. 

Open methods include:
* __Open protocols__, which are standalone protocols shared through dedicated repositories or published as open access articles.
* __Open measures for behavioural assessment__, such as open questionnaires and interviews shared independently or as part of open access articles and of key importance in mental health research.
* __Open software__, such as programs and code for organizing and processing data or running experiments.
* __Open hardware__, such as open designs for equipment or tools for experimental research.

__Clear and detailed methods are more reusable, reproducible, and credible__: they limit pitfalls related to scientific misunderstanding, saving time and resources. __Open methods, by being more discoverable and accessible, [bring these advantages a step further](https://theplosblog.plos.org/2021/07/methods-as-scientific-asset/)__ and support [responsible research practices](doi.org/10.1080/08989621.2023.2189010). As such, open methods facilitate replicating and understanding findings, as well as devising new applications of methodologies.

## A gateway to open science?

Documenting your methods in detail, if not already routine, brings benefits even within your own research group. Additionally, __open methods can be shared regardless of a project’s data being made open__. This is particularly pertinent when data sharing is limited by ownership constraints or sensitivity. Standalone methods articles are also among the most highly-cited category of publications and often remain relevant for longer than standard research articles.
