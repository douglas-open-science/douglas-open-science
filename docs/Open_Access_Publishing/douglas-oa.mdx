# The Douglas Research Centre Open Access Policy

__In 2021, the Douglas Research Centre adopted the first version of its Open Access policy__. This policy mirrors the __[Open access policy of the Fonds de recherche du Québec (FRQ) published in 2019](https://douglas.research.mcgill.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/politique-libre-acces_en_avril19.pdf)__. Under the current policy, self-archiving of accepted manuscripts and open access achieved through journals are both acceptable pathways, as long as publications are made openly available within 12 months of publications.

Our policy provides contextualized information on how to achieve open access publishing goals within the Douglas Research Centre’s specific environment:

We note that FRQ has recently [updated its open access policy to align with Plan S](https://douglas.research.mcgill.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/politique-libre-acces-revisee_vf.pdf), and will start mandating this new policy for some grant competitions launched in July 2022. While we commit to updating our guidance and policy in due time, __we advise authors to consult the [FRQ website](https://frq.gouv.qc.ca/politique_diffusion_2019/) to determine which policy applies to their specific situations__. In addition, authors funded by additional organizations, such as the __[Tri-Agency](https://www.ic.gc.ca/eic/site/063.nsf/eng/h_F6765465.html)__, are always recommended to compare the different open access policies mandated by each funder.
