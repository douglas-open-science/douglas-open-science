# Training resources and opportunities
The Creative Commons organization offers [various training programs](https://creativecommons.org/about/training-and-consulting/training/), including a 10-week asynchronous course.

For a brief introduction to CC licenses, please refer to [“What is "open data" anyway? An introduction to licensing and copyright for research data in Canada”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQl6d3oid84&ab_channel=TheAlliance%7CL%E2%80%99Alliance), a workshop presented by Shanna Hollich from Creative Commons and hosted by the Digital Research Alliance of Canada.

McGill library frequently offers training on Open Science practices, including on the use of open licenses. Please consult [their calendar](https://libraryrooms.mcgill.ca/calendar/mcgill) for the next scheduled events.

:::guide[Need help?]
#### Douglas Reseach Centre
The [Douglas Open Science Team](mailto:open.science.ouverte.comtl@ssss.gouv.qc.ca) can provide general information about open licenses and refer you to examples of local usage.
#### McGill University
The McGill Library can assist you in selecting the appropriate open license for your research outputs: find its guide on copyright and common open licenses as well as contact details for the copyright librarian [here](https://libraryguides.mcgill.ca/c.php?g=710276&p=5060547).
:::
