# Accessing open access publications
__Open access publications can be accessed for no cost (i.e. with no access fee or paid subscription necessary) regardless of whether browsing through a university network connection or not__. This ease of access [__largely benefits non-academic audiences__](https://zenodo.org/record/4143313#.YuLkQHbMIuW), including educators, healthcare professionals, and policymakers. In the case of the Douglas community, learning to find open access publications is particularly relevant for those not affiliated with McGill University.

# Practical steps 

- Find articles that are openly available within journals by directly accessing the publisher’s website. 
- Locate self-archived accepted manuscripts using tools that point readers to the best openly available versions of a publication, such as [Unpaywall’s browser extension](https://unpaywall.org/products/extension),  [Dimensions](https://app.dimensions.ai/discover/publication), and [Open Alex](https://openalex.org/). In addition, repositories such as eScholarship@McGill are indexed by Google Scholar, allowing accepted manuscripts to be easily discoverable through your usual search processes – if conducting a Google Scholar search, click on the “All … versions” option to determine if any of the alternative versions of the publication are openly accessible.
