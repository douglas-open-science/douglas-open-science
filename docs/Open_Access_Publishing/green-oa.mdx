# Self-archiving accepted manuscripts

## What is green open access?

Self-archiving accepted manuscripts, also called self-archiving post-prints or green open access, is __the practice of publishing an article in a journal of choice, and in parallel depositing the accepted version of the publication in an open access repository__ (e.g., [__eScholarship@McGill__](https://escholarship.mcgill.ca/)). This pathway is typically free of charge and compliant with most open access policies, as long as embargo periods and copyright considerations imposed by publishers are compatible with funders’ expectations.

__Accepted manuscripts are the final version of articles before production by the publisher and contain all edits made as a result of the peer review process__. Typically, publishers restrict the versions of the publication that can be deposited, and the final, published versions cannot be used for this purpose.

__Self-archiving accepted manuscripts may also be used as a strategy to retroactively open publications__ – it is never too late to collect previously accepted manuscripts and forward them to [__eScholarship@McGill__](https://escholarship.mcgill.ca/)!

## Practical steps

1. __Prior to submission, verify if embargo periods and copyright restrictions imposed by publishers for self-archiving are compatible with relevant open access policies__. This information can be found directly on the publisher’s website or summarized in [Jisc Open policy finder](https://openpolicyfinder.jisc.ac.uk/). Need help using Jisc Open policy finder? Check out [this page](https://www.mcgill.ca/libraries/research-services/open-access/tri-agency/check-journal-compliance) by the McGill Libraries.
2. __Submit your manuscript as usual.__
3. __Send accepted manuscripts to eScholarship@McGill__ using their email address ([escholarship.library@mcgill.ca](mailto:escholarship.library@mcgill.ca)) or [deposit form](https://www.mcgill.ca/libraries/research-services/escholarship/how-submit/form).

__Tip: act on acceptance!__ Create a habit of always saving your accepted manuscripts and forwarding them to [eScholarship@McGill](https://escholarship.mcgill.ca/) right upon acceptance. There is no need to wait for the expiration of embargo periods, as their team will handle those for you.
