# Training resources and opportunities
__McGill Libraries frequently offer training on open access publishing and other open science practices__. Please consult [their calendar](https://libraryrooms.mcgill.ca/calendar/mcgill) for the next scheduled events. We also invite you to watch recorded versions of the following webinars by Scholarly Communications Librarian, Jessica Lange:

- [__“How to Make Your Work Open Access: psst it doesn’t have to cost money”__](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=shsgzKrgpYM&ab_channel=McGillLibrary)
- [__“Preprints: What, Why, How”__](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jAcc3nQ3FvM)
- [__“Publier en libre accès”__](https://youtu.be/ees_8e5x7es)

__To learn more about the academic, economic and societal impacts of open access, we recommend [this review](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.8460.3) as a starting point.__

:::guide[Need help?]
#### Douglas Reseach Centre
The [Douglas Open Science Team](mailto:open.science.ouverte.comtl@ssss.gouv.qc.ca) can provide information about Open Access publishing, as well as help you identify the best solutions for specific publications. We can also analyze the Open Access status of your complete publications list and assist you in retroactively opening closed publications.
#### McGill University
Researchers are invited to contact the McGill Libraries for [further resources](https://www.mcgill.ca/libraries/research-services/open-access) and specific support for navigating eScholarship@McGill repository or APC discounts (find contact information for your liaison librarian or the scholarly communications librarian [here](https://www.mcgill.ca/libraries/contact-us/liaison)).
:::
