# Open access through journals

## How does making publications open access through journals work?

__Publications can be made open access through journals, usually by paying article processing charges (APCs)__. While APCs are generally expensive ($1,000-$10,000), articles are often openly available at the time of publication. In many cases, [__APC discounts are available through McGill University__](https://search.scifree.se/mcgill).

Open access jargon frequently includes words such as “gold”, “diamond”, “platinum”, “bronze” and “hybrid” to describe different options for achieving open access through journals:

### Gold open access publishing
__Gold journals__ only publish open access publications and require authors to pay APCs.

### Diamond or platinum open access publishing
__Diamond or platinum journals__ allow open access publications but do not require payment of APCs (these journals are unfortunately rare in the life and health sciences).

### Hybrid or bronze open access publishing
__Hybrid or bronze journals__ allow both closed (subscription model, by not paying APCs) and paid open publications. While hybrid journals apply open licenses and facilitate the reuse of content, bronze journals may lack clearly identified licenses. Some funders aligned with [__Plan S__](https://www.coalition-s.org/plan_s_principles/), such as [FRQ](https://douglas.research.mcgill.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/politique-libre-acces-revisee_vf.pdf), are now restricting the eligibility of APC expenses for partially open journals such as these.

## Practical steps

1. Search the [__Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ)__](https://doaj.org/), to identify open access journals and associated article processing charges (APCs). DOAJ additionally identifies open access journals following a set of best practices criteria, empowering authors to fully comply with applicable policies and recognize predatory journals.
2. __Check for [article processing charges discounts](https://search.scifree.se/mcgill) through McGill University and/or ensure that APCs are an allowable expense through your funding__.
3. Select open access options with the publisher and ensure that APCs have been budgeted.
4. Optional: [self-archive your accepted manuscript](green-oa.mdx).
